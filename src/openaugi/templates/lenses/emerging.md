# Emerging

## Intent

This lens IS the snapshot agent, made addressable from every surface
("apply lens emerging", mobile chip, zzz). **Read and follow
`OpenAugi/AGENT/snapshot-agent.md`** — its Area Seeds, output structure,
and retrieval rules are the spec. Do not duplicate them here; that file
stays the single source of truth.

The five questions, for orientation (full versions there):

- What is active right now?
- What is trying to emerge but is not yet explicit?
- What pattern has appeared before?
- What old note changes how this recent evidence should be read?
- What is the smallest useful next action?

- Do not overfit from one recent note; recurring means multiple traces.
- OpenAugi/-sourced blocks are secondary evidence only.
